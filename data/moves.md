# Pokemon Competitive Moves

## Physical Moves

### Normal

- **Body Slam**: Normal | 85 BP | 100% Acc | 15 PP | Physical. 30% chance to paralyze the target.
- **Double-Edge**: Normal | 120 BP | 100% Acc | 15 PP | Physical. User takes 33% recoil damage.
- **Extreme Speed**: Normal | 80 BP | 100% Acc | 5 PP | Physical. Priority +2. Almost always goes first.
- **Facade**: Normal | 70 BP | 100% Acc | 20 PP | Physical. Power doubles if the user is burned, paralyzed, or poisoned.
- **Rapid Spin**: Normal | 50 BP | 100% Acc | 40 PP | Physical. Removes entry hazards, binding moves, and Leech Seed from the user's side.
- **Return**: Normal | 102 BP | 100% Acc | 20 PP | Physical. Max power at max friendship.

- **Sacred Fire**: Fire | 100 BP | 95% Acc | 5 PP | Physical. 50% chance to burn the target. Signature move of Ho-Oh.
- **V-Create**: Fire | 180 BP | 95% Acc | 5 PP | Physical. Lowers user's Defense, Sp. Def, and Speed by 1 stage.

## Key Status Moves

- **Stealth Rock**: Rock | — BP | —% Acc | 20 PP | Status. Sets entry hazard that damages switching Pokemon based on type effectiveness (12.5% to 50% HP). The most important competitive move.
- **Spikes**: Ground | — BP | —% Acc | 20 PP | Status. Entry hazard that damages grounded Pokemon switching in. Stacks up to 3 layers (12.5%, 16.7%, 25%).
- **Toxic Spikes**: Poison | — BP | —% Acc | 20 PP | Status. Entry hazard. 1 layer poisons, 2 layers badly poisons switching grounded Pokemon.
- **Sticky Web**: Bug | — BP | —% Acc | 20 PP | Status. Entry hazard that lowers Speed of grounded Pokemon by 1 stage on switch-in.
- **Defog**: Flying | — BP | —% Acc | 15 PP | Status. Removes all entry hazards from both sides. Also removes screens.
- **Toxic**: Poison | — BP | 90% Acc | 10 PP | Status. Badly poisons the target (damage increases each turn).
- **Will-O-Wisp**: Fire | — BP | 85% Acc | 15 PP | Status. Burns the target, halving their Attack.
- **Thunder Wave**: Electric | — BP | 90% Acc | 20 PP | Status. Paralyzes the target, halving their Speed.
- **Swords Dance**: Normal | — BP | —% Acc | 20 PP | Status. Raises user's Attack by 2 stages.
- **Calm Mind**: Psychic | — BP | —% Acc | 20 PP | Status. Raises user's Sp. Atk and Sp. Def by 1 stage.
- **Quiver Dance**: Bug | — BP | —% Acc | 20 PP | Status. Raises user's Sp. Atk, Sp. Def, and Speed by 1 stage. One of the best setup moves.
- **Roost**: Flying | — BP | —% Acc | 5 PP | Status. Heals 50% of max HP. User loses Flying type for the turn.
- **Recover**: Normal | — BP | —% Acc | 5 PP | Status. Heals 50% of max HP.
- **Wish**: Normal | — BP | —% Acc | 10 PP | Status. Heals 50% of user's max HP at the end of the next turn. Can heal a switch-in.
- **Protect**: Normal | — BP | —% Acc | 10 PP | Status. Blocks all attacks for one turn. Fails if used consecutively. Essential in doubles.
- **Substitute**: Normal | — BP | —% Acc | 10 PP | Status. User loses 25% HP to create a decoy that absorbs hits.
- **Trick Room**: Psychic | — BP | —% Acc | 5 PP | Status. For 5 turns, slower Pokemon move first. Reverses speed tiers.
- **Tailwind**: Flying | — BP | —% Acc | 15 PP | Status. Doubles the Speed of the user's team for 4 turns.
- **Teleport**: Psychic | — BP | —% Acc | 20 PP | Status. Priority -6. User switches out. Goes last, ensuring safe switch.
