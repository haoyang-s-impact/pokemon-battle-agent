# Pokemon Competitive Strategy Guide

## Core Mechanics

### STAB (Same-Type Attack Bonus)

When a Pokemon uses a move that matches one of its own types, the move's power is multiplied by 1.5x. For example, a Water-type Pokemon using Surf (Water move) gets STAB, effectively making it 135 base power instead of 90. STAB is one of the most important factors in competitive play because it makes a Pokemon's same-type moves significantly stronger than coverage moves.

### Type Effectiveness

Type matchups are the foundation of competitive Pokemon. A super effective hit deals 2x damage, while a resisted hit deals 0.5x damage. Immune matchups deal 0x damage. With dual types, these multiply: a Fire/Flying Pokemon hit by a Rock move takes 4x damage (2x from Fire weakness * 2x from Flying weakness). Understanding these matchups determines which Pokemon to switch in and which moves to use.

### Physical vs Special Split

Every move is either Physical (uses Attack vs Defense), Special (uses Sp. Atk vs Sp. Def), or Status (no damage). Every Pokemon has different Attack and Sp. Atk stats, so effective teambuilding matches each Pokemon's higher offensive stat with appropriate moves. A Pokemon with 130 Attack and 60 Sp. Atk should use physical moves, not special ones.

## Competitive Concepts

### Checks vs Counters

A **counter** to a Pokemon is something that can switch into any of its moves and then threaten to KO or force it out. A **check** is a Pokemon that can beat it in a direct 1v1 but cannot safely switch in. For example, Landorus-Therian counters most Electric-types because it's immune to Electric moves (Ground type) and resists their common coverage. A faster Pokemon that KOs but can't take a hit is only a check.

### Momentum and Pivoting

Momentum refers to which player has the initiative -- the ability to force favorable trades or force switches. Pivoting moves like U-Turn, Volt Switch, and Flip Turn are critical because they deal damage AND switch the user out, letting you bring in the right counter while keeping pressure on the opponent. A "VoltTurn" core (one Pokemon with Volt Switch, another with U-Turn) is a classic competitive strategy for maintaining momentum.

### Entry Hazards

Entry hazards (Stealth Rock, Spikes, Toxic Spikes, Sticky Web) damage or status Pokemon when they switch in. They are the most important strategic element in competitive play:

- **Stealth Rock** is on nearly every competitive team. It deals type-based damage: 12.5% to neutral Pokemon, 25% to Pokemon weak to Rock (like Fire, Flying), and a devastating 50% to 4x weak Pokemon (like Volcarona, Charizard). This means Pokemon like Charizard lose half their HP just by switching in.
- **Spikes** stack up to 3 layers, dealing 12.5%, 16.7%, and 25% max HP per switch. They only affect grounded Pokemon.
- **Hazard removal** via Rapid Spin or Defog is essential. Most teams carry a hazard remover, and the battle over setting vs removing hazards is a central competitive dynamic.

### Setup Sweeping

Setup moves (Swords Dance, Nasty Plot, Dragon Dance, Quiver Dance, Calm Mind) boost a Pokemon's stats, allowing it to overwhelm the opponent's team. The strategy:

1. Remove or weaken the sweeper's counters first.
2. Bring in the sweeper on a forced switch or weak attack.
3. Use one or two setup moves.
4. Sweep through the remaining team with boosted stats.

Dragon Dance is particularly dangerous because it boosts both Attack and Speed, letting a Pokemon outspeed and overpower threats simultaneously. Quiver Dance boosts Sp. Atk, Sp. Def, and Speed, making the user both faster and bulkier while hitting harder.

### Weather

Weather conditions affect the battlefield and power up certain strategies:

- **Rain** (set by Drizzle ability or Rain Dance): Water moves deal 1.5x damage, Fire moves deal 0.5x damage. Thunder and Hurricane become 100% accurate. Swift Swim doubles the Speed of Pokemon with that ability.
- **Sun** (set by Drought ability or Sunny Day): Fire moves deal 1.5x damage, Water moves deal 0.5x damage. Chlorophyll doubles Speed. Solar Beam charges instantly.
- **Sand** (set by Sand Stream ability or Sandstorm): Deals 6.25% chip damage per turn to non-Rock/Ground/Steel types. Raises the Sp. Def of Rock-types by 1.5x. Sand Rush doubles Speed.
- **Snow** (set by Snow Warning ability): Raises the Defense of Ice-types by 1.5x. Aurora Veil can be set in snow, reducing all damage by roughly 33%.

Weather wars -- the battle over which weather is active -- are a major strategic element. Teams built around weather (rain teams, sun teams) try to keep their weather active while disrupting the opponent's.

### Wallbreaking

Wallbreakers are Pokemon with extremely high offensive stats or powerful moves designed to break through defensive Pokemon ("walls"). They often use moves with drawbacks (Overheat, Draco Meteor, Close Combat) because raw power matters more than sustainability. The idea: the wallbreaker creates holes in the opponent's defensive core, then a faster sweeper cleans up.

### Defensive Cores

Good teams don't just have six offensive threats. They build defensive synergy:

- **Fire/Water/Grass core**: Each resists the other's weaknesses. Water resists Fire, Grass resists Water, Fire resists Grass.
- **Steel + Fairy**: Steel resists Dragon and Fairy's weaknesses (Poison, Steel). Fairy covers Steel's Fighting weakness.
- **Ground + Flying**: Ground is immune to Electric (which threatens Flying). Flying is immune to Ground (which threatens... Ground). Together they cover each other's weaknesses well.

### Speed Control

Controlling who moves first is critical:

- **Choice Scarf**: Held item that boosts Speed by 1.5x but locks the user into one move. Used to turn slower Pokemon into revenge killers.
- **Priority moves** (Aqua Jet, Bullet Punch, Ice Shard, Mach Punch, Shadow Sneak, Extreme Speed, Sucker Punch): These always go before normal moves regardless of Speed. Extreme Speed has +2 priority and is the strongest priority move.
- **Trick Room**: Reverses speed for 5 turns so the slowest Pokemon moves first. Entire teams can be built around this.
- **Tailwind**: Doubles the team's Speed for 4 turns. Common in doubles.
- **Paralysis**: Thunder Wave and other paralysis moves halve the target's Speed permanently, effectively making them slower than most of the metagame.

### Prediction and Mindgames

Competitive Pokemon is fundamentally about prediction. Both players choose their actions simultaneously:

- If you predict the opponent will switch their Water type into your Electric move, you can use a Grass move instead to hit the incoming switch.
- If you predict a switch, you can set up (use Swords Dance or Stealth Rock) instead of attacking.
- Double switching -- switching yourself to gain a type advantage against the opponent's predicted switch-in -- is an advanced technique.
- The key principle: don't always make the "obvious" play. If your opponent expects you to switch, staying in with coverage can catch them off guard.
