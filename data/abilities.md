# Pokemon Competitive Abilities

## Weather-Setting Abilities

- **Drizzle**: Summons Rain for 5 turns when the Pokemon enters battle. Rain boosts Water moves by 50%, weakens Fire moves by 50%, and makes Thunder and Hurricane 100% accurate. Pokemon: Pelipper, Kyogre.
- **Drought**: Summons Sun for 5 turns when the Pokemon enters battle. Sun boosts Fire moves by 50%, weakens Water moves by 50%, and allows Solar Beam to fire instantly. Pokemon: Torkoal, Groudon.
- **Sand Stream**: Summons Sandstorm for 5 turns when the Pokemon enters battle. Sandstorm deals 6.25% chip damage per turn to non-Rock/Ground/Steel types and raises Rock-type Sp. Def by 50%. Pokemon: Tyranitar, Hippowdon.
- **Snow Warning**: Summons Snow for 5 turns when the Pokemon enters battle. Snow raises Ice-type Defense by 50% and enables Aurora Veil. Pokemon: Alolan Ninetales, Abomasnow.

## Terrain-Setting Abilities

- **Electric Surge**: Sets Electric Terrain for 5 turns. Boosts Electric moves by 30% and prevents grounded Pokemon from falling asleep. Pokemon: Tapu Koko, Pincurchin.
- **Grassy Surge**: Sets Grassy Terrain for 5 turns. Boosts Grass moves by 30%, heals grounded Pokemon 1/16 HP per turn, and halves Earthquake/Bulldoze damage. Pokemon: Tapu Bulu, Rillaboom.
- **Misty Surge**: Sets Misty Terrain for 5 turns. Halves Dragon move damage against grounded Pokemon and prevents status conditions on grounded Pokemon. Pokemon: Tapu Fini.
- **Psychic Surge**: Sets Psychic Terrain for 5 turns. Boosts Psychic moves by 30% and blocks priority moves against grounded Pokemon. Pokemon: Tapu Lele, Indeedee.

## Offensive Abilities

- **Adaptability**: Increases STAB bonus from 1.5x to 2x. Makes same-type moves devastatingly powerful. Pokemon: Porygon-Z, Crawdaunt.
- **Huge Power / Pure Power**: Doubles the Pokemon's Attack stat. Turns otherwise weak Pokemon into fearsome physical attackers. Pokemon: Azumarill (Huge Power), Medicham (Pure Power).
- **Protean / Libero**: Changes the Pokemon's type to match the move it's about to use, granting STAB on every attack. Pokemon: Greninja (Protean), Cinderace (Libero). Both nerfed in Gen 9 to only activate once per switch-in.
- **Sheer Force**: Removes secondary effects of moves but boosts their damage by 30%. Pairs well with Life Orb (no recoil). Pokemon: Nidoking, Darmanitan.
- **Tough Claws**: Boosts contact moves by 30%. Pokemon: Mega Metagross, Mega Charizard X.
- **Beast Boost**: Raises the Pokemon's highest stat by 1 stage after KOing an opponent. Enables snowball sweeping. Pokemon: Ultra Beasts (Pheromosa, Kartana, etc.).

## Defensive Abilities

- **Intimidate**: Lowers the Attack of all opposing Pokemon by 1 stage upon entry. One of the most impactful abilities in both singles and doubles. Pokemon: Landorus-Therian, Incineroar, Gyarados.
- **Multiscale / Shadow Shield**: Halves damage taken when at full HP. Extremely powerful for setup. Pokemon: Dragonite (Multiscale), Lugia (Multiscale), Lunala (Shadow Shield).
- **Regenerator**: Heals 33% HP when switching out. Enables very aggressive pivoting playstyles. Pokemon: Slowbro, Slowking, Toxapex, Mienshao.
- **Sturdy**: Prevents being KOed from full HP in one hit (like a built-in Focus Sash). Pokemon: Skarmory, Magnezone.
- **Unaware**: Ignores the opponent's stat changes when taking or dealing damage. Shuts down setup sweepers. Pokemon: Clefable, Dondozo, Skeledirge.
- **Magic Bounce**: Reflects status moves (Stealth Rock, Toxic, Thunder Wave, etc.) back at the user. Pokemon: Hatterene, Mega Sableye.
- **Natural Cure**: Heals any status condition when the Pokemon switches out. Pokemon: Blissey, Starmie.

## Speed Control Abilities

- **Swift Swim**: Doubles Speed in Rain. Pokemon: Barraskewda, Kingdra.
- **Chlorophyll**: Doubles Speed in Sun. Pokemon: Venusaur, Lilligant.
- **Sand Rush**: Doubles Speed in Sandstorm. Pokemon: Excadrill, Dracozolt.
- **Slush Rush**: Doubles Speed in Snow. Pokemon: Beartic, Alolan Sandslash.
- **Speed Boost**: Raises Speed by 1 stage at the end of each turn. Eventually outspeeds everything. Pokemon: Blaziken, Ninjask.
- **Unburden**: Doubles Speed when the Pokemon's held item is consumed. Pairs with berries or Acrobatics. Pokemon: Hawlucha, Hitmonlee.

## Utility Abilities

- **Levitate**: Grants immunity to Ground-type moves. Extremely valuable since Earthquake is ubiquitous. Pokemon: Rotom forms, Gengar (pre-Gen 7), Latios, Latias.
- **Magic Guard**: Prevents all indirect damage (entry hazards, weather, poison, burn, Life Orb recoil). Pokemon: Clefable, Alakazam, Reuniclus.
- **Mold Breaker**: Ignores the target's ability when attacking. Hits Levitate Pokemon with Ground moves, ignores Sturdy, etc. Pokemon: Excadrill, Haxorus.
- **Prankster**: Gives +1 priority to status moves. Makes support moves like Thunder Wave, Taunt, and Will-O-Wisp very fast. Does not work on Dark types. Pokemon: Grimmsnarl, Whimsicott, Sableye.
- **Technician**: Boosts moves with 60 or less base power by 50%. Makes weak moves like Bullet Punch (40 -> 60 effective BP) and Mach Punch much stronger. Pokemon: Scizor, Breloom.
